Thank you for supporting Spectacular. Your subscription unlocks the full set of Pro features across the Figma plugin and the CLI, and it funds the work that keeps both moving. If anything about your license or your billing looks wrong, we would rather hear about it than have you work around it.

## Specs Pro

Your subscription carries two license keys — one for the Figma plugin and one for the CLI. Both live in your Polar customer portal; neither is emailed to you.

- Sign in to the [Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/directed-edges-llc/portal) with the email address you used at checkout — Polar sends you a one-time login link.
- Copy the plugin key into the **Account** pane of the [Spectacular plugin](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1549454283615386215/specs-2-formerly-anova) to activate Pro in Figma.
- Copy the CLI key into a `.env` file in your project as `SPECS_LICENSE_KEY`, or pass it to any command with `-l`. The [CLI getting started guide](/cli/getting-started/) covers both.
- Each key is meant for one person and activates on up to two machines.

What each tier includes, how CLI generations are metered, and the full subscription terms are on the [licensing page](/overview/licensing/).

## Specs Pro for Teams

A Teams subscription gives you a pool of seats rather than a set of keys. Nobody holds a license until a seat is assigned to their email address and they claim it — and that includes you, the administrator.

- Sign in to the [Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/directed-edges-llc/portal), choose **Manage your subscription**, then **Invite members** — once for each seat you bought.
- Look for your own address in the seat list. If it isn't there, assign yourself a seat: paying for the subscription does not license the administrator.
- Each person you invite receives an email with a link to claim their seat. Claim links expire after 24 hours and can be re-sent from the portal.
- Once a seat is claimed, that person's own plugin and CLI keys appear in their own Polar portal, and they activate them exactly as an individual subscriber does.
- A seat nobody has claimed yet still counts toward your seat total and is billed normally.

Seat counts, volume discounts, and the detail of how claiming works are on the [licensing page](/overview/licensing/).

## Manage your subscription

Use the [Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/directed-edges-llc/portal) to view receipts, update your payment method, change your billing email, or cancel your subscription. It's also where you download invoices for expensing. Sign in with the email used at checkout — Polar sends you a one-time login link.

All seat management happens in the customer portal alongside the rest of your subscription. You can see which seats are claimed, revoke a seat when someone leaves and reassign it to a new email, and adjust the total seat count up or down at any time. Changes are prorated on your month-to-month invoice — adding a seat mid-cycle adds a partial charge, removing one issues a partial credit, and volume discount tiers re-evaluate automatically whenever your seat count crosses a threshold.
